Reconstruct a scene from photographs

Capture overlapping views, estimate the cameras, and inspect a coloured 3D point cloud.

1. Add photographs

Walk around a still, textured object. Keep neighbouring views about 70–80% overlapping. Take 12–30 sharp photographs; translate the camera rather than only turning it.

JPEG or PNG · 3–80 images · up to 256 MB

iPhone: use Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or export HEIC photos as JPEG. Photos stay on the machine running this server.
